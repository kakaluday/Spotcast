# Spotcast Brand Guidelines — Pending Asset Verification

**Non-negotiable:** preserve Spotcast's existing approved brand identity.

## To capture from original sources
- Primary logo and permitted variants
- Exact font family, font files/licences and weights
- Primary, secondary and neutral HEX colours
- Button, link, heading and body typography rules
- Photography and iconography examples
- Voice and copy examples

Do not invent or substitute brand tokens until verified. Store licensed assets only where repository access and licence terms permit; this repository is public.

## Design direction
Premium, clear, confident, locally relevant and mobile-first. Use authentic screen and venue photography, accessible contrast and restrained animation.
