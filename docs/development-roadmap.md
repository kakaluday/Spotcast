# Development Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Discovery (weeks 1–2)
Audit existing site and assets; document business needs, network data, hosting and integrations.

## Phase 2 — UX and brand (weeks 3–5)
Confirm exact typography and colour tokens; design sitemap, user flows and wireframes.

## Phase 3 — UI design (weeks 5–8)
Create responsive Figma prototype and component system; approve photography direction.

## Phase 4 — Core website (weeks 8–14)
Build frontend, CMS, pages, forms, SEO and analytics.

## Phase 5 — Network and planner (weeks 12–18)
Implement verified inventory schema, map, search filters, shortlist and structured campaign enquiry.

## Phase 6 — QA and launch (weeks 18–22)
Content, accessibility, security, performance, redirects, testing and controlled launch.

## Phase 7 — Optimisation (weeks 22–24+)
Review conversions and consider bookings, accounts and reporting after operational readiness.

### Release boundaries
1. Premium marketing website
2. Inventory explorer and enquiry platform
3. Advanced availability, booking and reporting

Dates are indicative and require confirmation of scope and resourcing.
