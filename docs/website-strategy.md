# Spotcast Website Strategy

## Vision
Create a premium, accessible New Zealand local digital screen advertising platform. Benchmark the quality of JCDecaux NZ and MediaWorks NZ while retaining Spotcast's own brand identity.

## Audiences
1. Advertisers and agencies exploring advertising opportunities.
2. Local businesses seeking simple campaign packages.
3. Venue owners considering a screen partnership.

## Core propositions
- Advertising in relevant local venues.
- Simple campaign planning and clear enquiries.
- Creative support and venue partnerships.
- Evidence-based audience and network information.

## Core experiences
- Premium homepage and product pages.
- Searchable screen network with privacy-aware venue profiles.
- Campaign shortlist and guided enquiry builder.
- Campaign examples and case studies.
- Venue partner recruitment and applications.
- Insights, FAQs, media kit and contact.

## Principles
- Keep existing approved fonts, colours and logo.
- Use genuine venue imagery and verified statistics.
- Do not claim live inventory availability until integrated.
- Prioritise mobile accessibility, SEO, speed and lead quality.
- Build incrementally and review changes via pull requests.

## Benchmarks
- https://www.jcdecaux.co.nz/
- https://www.mediaworks.co.nz/

This document is an initial planning brief, not a completed page-by-page technical audit.
